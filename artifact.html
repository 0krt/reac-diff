characters redraw the frame as a grid of glyphs from a small set, each cell picking the glyph whose weight matches the field there. false colour maps the level straight onto a scientific colour scale. isolines draw evenly spaced contours of the level over any of it. the elevation overlay prints the level in each cell as a digit, 0 at range low to 9 at range high. all three read on the flat field and on a projected object, and png / video export capture them.

Liquid

liquid turns the pattern itself to poured metal — it drifts on a slow flow and mirrors a small environment tinted by the palette, while the ground around it stays matte. pulse takes its phase from the field, so the waves run along the pattern rather than over the frame. both read on the flat field and on a projected object.
